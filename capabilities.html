Our process

From first conversation to lasting stewardship.

Every project moves through five phases. Timelines vary by scope, but this is the rhythm most of our coastal estate projects follow.

01

Consultation

1-2 weeks

Site walk, scope conversation, and initial fit call. Free and no obligation.

02

Design

4-8 weeks

Site survey, master plan, planting scheme, 3D renderings, and cost estimates.

03

Approval

2-3 weeks

Final scope, budget, schedule. Permits where needed. Then we go to order.

04

Build

8-20 weeks

Hardscape, grading, planting, and finish work - executed by our in-house crew.

05

Steward

Ongoing

Seasonal care plan to keep the landscape true to its original design intent.
