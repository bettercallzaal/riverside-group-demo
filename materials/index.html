How the cost columns get built

The math.

Every row: Cost (your unit cost from supplier) × Markup % = Customer Price. Markup percentages follow industry norms by category - commodities run leaner, plants and stone run higher because they tie up cash and break.

The cost numbers in this draft are placeholders from published 2025 references (GreenMargins, Hammond Lumber catalog estimates). Once you verify against your last three invoices, those become the floor for every estimate going forward.
