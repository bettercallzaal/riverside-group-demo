Part one - your nine asks

What you asked for, mapped out.

Your SMS list, in your order. Each one with what I'd actually build, what ships, and which phase it lands in.

01
Tools & inventory
Phase 2

Tool & inventory tracking

"Trackers on certain high value tools and easy way to log use of tools/equipment on job sites."

Every tool gets a QR sticker. High-value gear gets an AirTag tied to the same record. Crew scans on check-out from the shop, scans on return. You see who has what, where, anytime - on your phone.

For the things that walk off without anyone noticing: a weekly summary lands in your inbox listing anything not seen in seven days, plus anything high-value off-property.

What ships
  • Tool list with QR + AirTag tags, photo, purchase date, value
  • Scan-to-check-in/out from any phone, no app install
  • Weekly "where's the gear" digest to you and Josh
02
Daily log
Phase 2

Daily log + 6pm digest

"Daily log/update that shares to me and company for task tracking, purchasing, invoicing, etc."

One quick form per crew member at end of day - what jobs they were on, hours, what got done, photos if any. Takes ninety seconds on a phone.

At 6pm every day, you get one email: who logged, who didn't, hours by job, purchases entered, anything flagged. Stops the "what happened today" phone calls.

What ships
  • Mobile-first end-of-day form (iPhone and Android)
  • Hours roll into the job they were worked on
  • 6pm digest email - skip the normal, dig into what's flagged

Website

Website refresh

"Website update/refresh."

Already built. Take a look at the demo home and the capabilities page - moss and brass, Fraunces type, mobile-first, fast on coastal signal.

If the direction is right, the rest is real photography, copy polish, and DNS. About a week to a clean launch.

What ships
  • Home + capabilities deep-dive + portfolio + journal + service area
  • Real project photography swapped for placeholders
  • Inquiry form wired to you and Josh
  • riversidegroupme.com pointed at the new site when you say go
04
Logo & brand
Phase 1 - this week

New logo + merch pack

"New logo to get shirts, hats, jackets, signage, business cards ordered."

The blocker for everything you print. Three directions to react to - refined-coastal, heritage-craft, rugged-modern. Pick one, we refine. Full pack: primary mark, secondary, monogram, color system, type pairing.

Then print-ready files for shirts, hats, jackets, truck signage, business cards. I coordinate the printer. You approve proofs.

What ships
  • 3 logo directions in roughly the first week
  • Refined final mark + full brand pack
  • Print-ready files for every merch item
  • Vendor coordination through approved proofs
05
Intake & work orders
Phase 2

Client intake + work orders

"New client intake info system and work order system to track timing and estimates, and keep new leads from falling through cracks."

Lead comes in - website form, phone call, referral, anywhere - lands in one board. Each has status, value, last-touch date. Anything sitting more than two days without action goes red. Morning email so nothing slips.

Convert a won lead to a job in one click. Auto PO number, ready for purchases and time to start tracking.

What ships
  • Lead board with status, value, last touch
  • Stale-lead alerts
  • One-click lead-to-job with auto PO numbering
06
Advertising
Phase 3

Advertising - clients & hiring

"Advertising for both employees and new clients."

Two separate funnels. For clients: targeted Meta + Houzz + local SEO around Mount Desert and coastal Hancock County. For hiring: Indeed, MDI community channels, and a College of the Atlantic pipeline.

Built on the new brand pack so everything looks like Riverside, not stock contractor ads.

What ships
  • Client acquisition: Meta + Houzz creative + local SEO
  • Hiring: Indeed posting + community board templates + COA outreach
  • Monthly check-in on what's working, what to kill
07
Equipment
Phase 2

Equipment maintenance

"Equipment maintenance schedule, tracking, etc (when and how to grease excavator, oil changes, safety checks)."

Each piece of equipment gets a record - excavator, trucks, trailers, mowers. Hour-meter readings come in from the crew. The system computes what's due, coming up, overdue. Big dashboard tile on the home screen.

Before the excavator goes on a job, the operator runs a short safety check from their phone. Pass, it goes. Fail, you get notified.

What ships
  • Equipment list with hour meters + service schedules
  • Pre-use safety check form for the excavator and tracked assets
  • Due / overdue dashboard so service stops being last-minute
08
Profit per job
Phase 2

Budgets, POs, profit per job

"Simple work order system that sets budgets and PO # for tracking purchases, hours, and just keeps better track of profit margins for each job."

Every job has a budget you set when you win it. Every purchase the crew makes - Home Depot, suppliers - gets snapped on a phone, tagged to the job, PO attaches automatically. Every hour from the daily log adds against the job.

Open the job, see one number: where you are on margin, live. No spreadsheet. No surprises at the end.

What ships
  • Auto PO numbering at job creation
  • Mobile receipt capture - photo, category, amount, done
  • Live budget vs. spent vs. margin on every job page
  • You and Josh see the money - crew sees scope, configurable
09
Business phone
Phase 1 - this week

Business phone + call routing

"Set up my new phone number. Press one for Cameron, 2 for billing (Josh) etc."

Fix the battery / charging issue at the Apple Store first. Then we set up the business number with a proper menu: press 1 for you, press 2 for Josh. You can answer from your existing iPhone without juggling a second device.

One business number on signs, cards, ads, everywhere. Calls route to the right person without you in the middle.

What ships
  • Phone hardware fixed
  • Business line live with menu routing
  • Calls and voicemails forward to existing devices
  • Unified number across all marketing surfaces
Part two - what I'd add

Four more I'd build in.

Your nine cover the surfaces you feel every day. These four cover what bites you when you least expect it - audits, accountant requests, missing certificates of insurance, clients asking where their project is. Quietly important. Below the waterline.

10
Compliance Added
Phase 2

Insurance, COIs, compliance

Why this matters: high-end clients ask for certificates of insurance before letting you on the property. Workers comp lapses cost real money. OSHA has rules for excavator operators. Right now this lives in your inbox and your head.

One place for every certificate: workers comp, general liability, auto, umbrella. Expiry dates with 30/60/90-day alerts so nothing lapses. Client COI requests answered in under 5 minutes by you, Josh, or a project manager.

Same place: contractor license, equipment operator certifications, sub-contractor 1099 records, safety incident log (in case you ever need it).

What ships
  • Insurance vault with COIs ready to send by job or by client
  • Expiry alerts before lapses bite
  • Sub-contractor + license + cert tracking in one drawer
  • Lightweight incident log for OSHA cover
11
Books Added
Phase 2

Books + financial integration

Why this matters: whatever Josh uses for the books (QuickBooks, Wave, accountant spreadsheet), the new system has to feed it cleanly. Otherwise Josh is doing double entry and hates everything we built.

Every PO, purchase, invoice, time entry exports clean to the accounting tool. End-of-month: one button, one export file, books closed without re-keying. End-of-year: 1099s for subs, tax-ready summary by job, by category.

This is the boring layer that decides if Josh actually adopts the system or quietly keeps using his spreadsheet.

What ships
  • QBO / accounting export per cadence Josh wants
  • 1099 tracking for sub-contractors
  • Tax-ready year-end packet by job and category
  • Josh's existing flow respected, not replaced
12
Client comms Added
Phase 3

Client communication + job updates

Why this matters: high-ticket landscape clients want to know what's happening on their property. Right now that's you texting photos at the end of long days. Doesn't scale, and you forget some.

Weekly client update goes out automatically per active job - this week's photos from the daily log, what got done, what's next. Branded, looks like Riverside. Cuts the "what's going on at my place" calls in half.

Change orders go out the same way - signed digitally, no chasing paper. Customers approve, work continues, money's tracked.

What ships
  • Weekly auto-generated client photo update per active job
  • Digital change-order signoff (one-click approve)
  • Public job-progress page if a client wants to share with family
13
Documents Added
Phase 2

Document + contract library

Why this matters: every job has a folder of stuff - signed proposal, change orders, permits, plant lists, irrigation as-builts, final photos, warranty info. Right now that's scattered across email, Google Drive, Cameron's truck, and Josh's filing cabinet.

One library, organized per job. Every document searchable. Every photo geotagged. Every permit linked to the city it came from. The next time a client asks "what kind of stone did you use on our terrace in 2024" - you find it in five seconds.

Also: estimate templates and material catalogs reusable across jobs, so you're not rebuilding from scratch each time.

What ships
  • Per-job document library, searchable
  • Reusable estimate templates + material catalog
  • Photo archive geotagged + tied to job
  • Permit / contract / signoff archive

Part three - the role

The terms I'd want.

What I'd take on, how I'd want it structured, and what I need from you to walk in clean. Open conversation - I'd rather we be direct about all of it now than figure it out month three.

Position

Operations lead

Build and run the 13-piece operating system above. Day-to-day ops point for the system, with you and Josh as the human escalation layer.

  • Title: Operations Manager or Operations Lead
  • Reports to: you
  • Direct interface with Josh on the books side
Compensation

$40/hr W-2

Annualized roughly $83,200 at 40 hours a week year-round. W-2 (not 1099) - clean for both of us tax-wise.

  • $40/hr, W-2 employee
  • Payroll cadence to confirm - weekly or bi-weekly
  • Overtime treatment past 40hrs/wk - matters during build
  • 90-day review built in - re-evaluate scope and rate once you've seen the system run
Healthcare

Health stipend

I lose my JAX coverage the day I leave. Need a plan from day one at Riverside. Standard small-business move: monthly stipend toward a marketplace plan I buy myself.

  • Ask: $500-800/mo stipend toward a CoverME.gov plan
  • Cheaper for you than a group plan, real money for me
  • Tax-friendly if we structure as a QSEHRA (decide later)
  • I'll bring a specific plan price to the next conversation
Retirement

Year-one bridge

I walk away from match dollars at JAX when I leave. To make year-one work, I'd want some bridge structure - we figure out the exact shape, but the principle is "don't lose ground on long-term savings."

  • Signing bonus, employer SEP-IRA contribution, or comp bump
  • Amount tied to what I leave behind at JAX (I'll confirm)
  • Sized to bridge the gap, not replicate full match forever
Transition

Clean handoff

Two-week notice at JAX, start at Riverside immediately after. No overlap - keeps things ethical and lets me focus.

  • ~2-3 week gap from today to start date
  • Any Riverside work I do before my JAX last-day stays on my own time
  • Written offer letter from you before I give notice
Scope

This is the job

The 13-piece system is the role - not a bonus expectation on top of crew work. Build hours and run hours both count. Confirming this so we're aligned on what success looks like.

  • Ops platform IS the job, not extra
  • Build phase and run phase both compensated
  • 90-day check-in on whether scope needs rebalancing
