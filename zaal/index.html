Tools & inventory
Phase 2

Tool & inventory tracking

"Trackers on certain high value tools and easy way to log use of tools/equipment on job sites."

Every tool gets a QR sticker. High-value gear gets an AirTag tied to the same record. Crew scans on check-out from the shop, scans on return. You see who has what, where, when - on your phone, anywhere.

For the things that walk off without anyone noticing: a Sunday-night summary lands in your inbox listing anything not seen in seven days, and anything high-value off-property.

What ships
  • Tool list with QR + AirTag tags, photo, purchase date, value
  • Scan-to-check-in/out from any phone, no app install
  • Weekly "where's the gear" digest to you and Josh
02
Daily log
Phase 2

Daily log + 6pm digest

"Daily log/update that shares to me and company for task tracking, purchasing, invoicing, etc."

One quick form per crew member at end of day - what jobs they were on, hours, what got done, photos if there are any. Takes ninety seconds on a phone.

At 6pm every day, you get one email: who logged, who didn't, hours by job, purchases entered, anything flagged. Stops the "what happened today" phone calls at night.

What ships
  • Mobile-first end-of-day form (works on iPhone and Android)
  • Hours roll automatically into the job they were worked on
  • 6pm digest email to you - skip anything that looks normal, dig into what's flagged

Intake & work orders
Phase 2

Client intake + work orders

"New client intake info system and work order system to track timing and estimates, and keep new leads from falling through cracks."

Lead comes in - website form, phone call, referral, anywhere - it lands in one board. Each lead has a status, a value, a last-touch date. Anything sitting more than two days without action goes red, and you get a morning email so nothing slips.

Convert a won lead to a job in one click. Job gets a PO number automatically, ready for purchases and time to start tracking against it.

What ships
  • Lead board with status, value, last touch
  • Stale-lead alerts so nothing sits in limbo
  • One-click lead-to-job with auto PO numbering

07
Equipment
Phase 2

Equipment maintenance

"Equipment maintenance schedule, tracking, etc (when and how to grease excavator, oil changes, safety checks)."

Each piece of equipment gets a record - excavator, trucks, trailers, mowers. Hour-meter readings come in from the crew. The system computes what's due, what's coming up, what's overdue. Big dashboard tile on the home screen.

Before the excavator goes on a job, the operator runs a six-question safety check from their phone. Pass, it goes. Fail, you get notified.

What ships
  • Equipment list with hour meters and service schedules
  • Pre-use safety check form for the excavator (and any other tracked asset)
  • Due / overdue dashboard so service stops being last-minute
08
Profit per job
Phase 2

Budgets, POs, profit per job

"Simple work order system that sets budgets and PO # for tracking purchases, hours, and just keeps better track of profit margins for each job."

Every job has a budget you set when you win it. Every purchase the crew makes - Home Depot, suppliers, anywhere - gets snapped on a phone, tagged to the job, the PO follows automatically. Every hour from the daily log adds up against the job.

Open the job and you see one number: where you are on margin, live. No spreadsheet. No surprises at the end.

What ships
  • Auto PO numbering at job creation
  • Mobile receipt capture - photo, category, amount, done
  • Live budget vs. spent vs. margin on every job page
  • For you and Josh only - crew sees scope, not money

Business phone
Phase 1 - this week

Business phone + call routing

"Set up my new phone number. Press one for Cameron, 2 for billing (Josh) etc."

Take the phone you have, fix the battery / charging issue at the Apple Store (about a hundred dollars or free if it's still under AppleCare). Then we set up the business number with a proper menu: press 1 for you, press 2 for Josh, press 3 for general voicemail, all routing to the right person.

You can answer from your existing iPhone without juggling a second device. Josh gets billing calls direct.

What ships
  • Phone hardware fixed
  • Business line live with menu routing
  • Calls and voicemails forward to existing devices
  • One business number on signs, cards, ads, everywhere
